Istanbul Arrival Logic

SAW → Taksim

The first Istanbul evening is best treated as one compact westbound city sweep. SAW is on the Asian side, so the handoff to Taksim is your main arrival friction point, not sightseeing density.

Best planning use Keep the first-night route narrow: hotel drop, Taksim, Istiklal, then Galata.
Why this helps It protects the next full city day and avoids turning the airport arrival into a sprawling crossing schedule.
SAW to Taksim Galata Tower

Historic Core Cluster

Walk-heavy day

Sultanahmet, the bazaars, and Balat read better as one anchored old-city day rather than separate destination blocks. The layout below already reflects that by compressing internal movement and leaving one cleaner westbound shift.

Natural anchor Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque establish the day before the market sequence.
Best late-day move Save Balat for the softer photo walk once the main landmark queue pressure is gone.
Hagia Sophia Historic core to Balat

Cappadocia Touring Base

Goreme / Uchisar arc

Cappadocia works best as a compact loop of short scenic drives around Goreme, Pasabag, Uchisar, and the main viewpoints, with one broader southbound day for Soganli and Kaymakli.

Good local split Use one day for north-and-central valleys, then a separate day for the underground city and southern drive.
What to avoid Mixing the southbound loop into the balloon-highlight day usually makes the schedule feel rushed.
Goreme base Goreme to Kaymakli

Kaş and Fethiye Coast

Road + sea stack

The coast reads as two different modes: Kaş for decompression and water access, then Oludeniz and Fethiye for a denser activity day. Keeping that difference visible helps the trip feel balanced.

Kaş role Recovery base after the longest inland transfer, with easier local movement and slower pace.
Fethiye role More active routing day, where paragliding and private boat timing decide how much else fits.

Important Items

Packing
